About Rentify PK

Rentify PK is a peer-to-peer rental marketplace built for Pakistan — connecting people who own items (electronics, furniture, vehicles, and more) with people who need them temporarily. The platform is built around CNIC-verified identities, escrow-protected payments, and rider-assisted delivery to make renting between strangers safe and simple.
