Cookie Policy

How Rentify PK keeps you signed in and remembers your preferences.

Last updated: {{ lastUpdated }}

This page explains how we keep you signed in — and, honestly, it's mostly local storage rather than traditional cookies.

1What We Actually Use

Rentify PK primarily uses your browser's local storage (not cookies) to keep you logged in. When you sign in, we store:

  • An access token and refresh token, so you don't have to log in on every visit.
  • Your basic profile info (name, role) so the interface can load instantly.

This data stays in your browser and is only sent to our own servers — it's not third-party ad tracking.

2Third-Party Cookies

Some features rely on third-party services that may set their own cookies when you use them, including:

  • Google / Facebook Sign-In — if you choose to log in with Google or Facebook.
  • Voice & video calling — our in-app calling feature may use session cookies to maintain a call.

We don't control these third-party cookies directly; they're governed by each provider's own privacy policy.

3Why We Use This

  • Keeping you logged in — so you don't have to re-enter your password every time.
  • Security — detecting suspicious login activity.
  • Preferences — remembering small things like your last-used filters.

4Controlling This Data

Logging out of Rentify PK clears your stored tokens from that browser. You can also clear your browser's local storage and cookies manually at any time through your browser settings — you'll just need to log in again afterward.
