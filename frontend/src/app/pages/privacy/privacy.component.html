Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information Rentify PK collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using Rentify PK, you agree to the practices described here.

1Information We Collect

Account details: name, email, phone number, and a securely hashed password (we never store your password in plain text).

Identity verification: when you verify your CNIC, we collect a photo of your CNIC and a selfie, which are processed to confirm your identity and are not shared publicly.

Listing & booking data: photos and descriptions you upload for listings, booking history, delivery addresses, and any inspection photos submitted during handovers.

Location data: approximate location for showing nearby listings, and live location for riders during an active delivery.

Payments & wallet: your wallet balance and transaction history within the app. We do not store raw card numbers on our servers.

Communications: messages, calls, and notifications sent through the platform between renters, owners, and riders.

2How We Use Your Information

  • To create and secure your account, and verify your identity for trust and safety.
  • To process bookings, payments, escrow releases, and refunds.
  • To connect renters, owners, and riders for deliveries and communication.
  • To calculate trust scores and badges based on your real activity and reviews.
  • To detect fraud, resolve disputes, and enforce our Terms of Service.
  • To send booking updates, delivery status, and (if you opt in) newsletter emails.

3Who We Share It With

We share only what's necessary to complete a booking — for example, a renter's delivery address is shared with the assigned rider, and an owner sees the renter's name and rating once a booking is confirmed. We do not sell your personal information to advertisers or third parties.

Some data is processed by trusted service providers on our behalf, including image hosting, voice/video calling infrastructure, and identity-verification tooling — each only receives what it needs to perform its specific function.

4Data Retention

We keep your account and booking records for as long as your account is active, and for a reasonable period afterward to meet legal, tax, and dispute-resolution obligations. CNIC images are retained only as long as needed for verification and fraud prevention.

5Your Rights

  • You can view and update most of your profile information from your account settings at any time.
  • You can request a copy of your data or ask us to delete your account by contacting support.
  • You can unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time using the link in those emails.

6Security

We use industry-standard measures — encrypted passwords, secure sessions, and access controls — to protect your information. No system is 100% secure, so we also encourage you to use a strong password and avoid sharing your login with anyone.

7Children's Privacy

Rentify PK is intended for users who are at least 18 years old (in line with CNIC eligibility). We do not knowingly collect information from minors.

8Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the platform evolves. We'll update the "Last updated" date above, and significant changes will be communicated through the app or by email.
